Lease basics

What forces a zombie machine off?

Why. Reclaim is tied to the lease itself — if renewals stop for any reason, the machine is shut down within one TTL. Nothing else needs to notice.

Why did the TTL ship at 15 minutes instead of 5?

Why. At 5 minutes, thousands of leases renewing together overloaded the keeper; 15 minutes trades slower reclaim for stability.
