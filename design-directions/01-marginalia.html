Philosophy

Three commitments

The margin is the teacher

A hover tooltip makes the reader choose between reading and understanding — you stop, aim, hover, read, lose your place. A visible sidenote costs one eye movement.1 Definitions, caveats, and context sit beside the sentence that needs them, so prose and explanation are absorbed together.

Ink before decoration

One serif for the material, one small sans for the apparatus, one pen-blue ink for the annotator's voice. Surfaces are hairlines on paper, never shadows. If an element doesn't help you learn, it isn't drawn.

Mark what matters

Emphasis works like a real highlighter: rare, yellow, and impossible to miss. One marked phrase per screen is a signal; five is noise. Bold is reserved for names and labels, not importance.

1Dual coding, by layout. Presenting an idea and its supporting context in adjacent, visually distinct channels lowers working-memory load — the reader never holds a definition in their head while parsing a sentence.

Iconography

Drawn with the same pen

Icons are line drawings on a 24-unit grid: 1.5px stroke, round caps, round joins — the weight of a fine pen, matching the serif's hairlines rather than competing with them. Always currentColor, so they inherit the voice they sit in.

Knowledge apparatus

Terms, depth, and self-checks

Jargon is marked with a dotted underline and a numbered reference: the build emits each tree-shaken1 definition into the margin, in reading order. Optional depth folds away in a paper-colored pleat:

How sidenote numbering works (optional depth)

Numbers restart at ¹ on every slide. The build collects rr-term elements in document order, assigns numbers, and emits matching notes into the slide's margin rail — authors never number by hand.

Self-check: where does a definition live in this system?

Why: hover hides knowledge behind an interaction and pins it to a cursor. The margin keeps the definition in peripheral vision, so the sentence and its explanation are read as one unit — that adjacency is the entire point of this direction.
1tree-shaken — only the pieces actually referenced are kept; unused icons and styles are dropped from the emitted file.
Self-checks keep their kit behavior — answers reveal instantly and results can be copied back to an agent. Only the clothing changes.

Specimen · a real lesson, set in Marginalia

The signature, isolated

One paragraph, fully annotated

When a debrief says the retry queue was idempotent1 but the dispatcher double-fired2 under load, the sentence you must not skim is this one: the bug was in the assumption, not the code — the queue honored its contract while the caller held a different contract3 in mind.

House rules: at most three sidenotes per slide, at most one highlighted phrase per screen. Scarcity is what keeps both legible as signals.

1idempotent — safe to run twice; the second run changes nothing.
2double-fired — dispatched the same job to two workers at once.
3contract — the behavior each side believes the other guarantees. Most incidents live in the gap between two of these.

Three changes ripple through the kit:

rr-term stops rendering tooltips and emits numbered margin notes; the doc shell gains the 17rem rail.
Body type moves to Iowan Old Style 18/1.7; sans becomes the apparatus-only voice.
Emphasis becomes the highlighter wash; accent consolidates on pen blue.

Cost of adoption: medium. Layout grid and rr-term change shape; all other components are re-skins of what exists.
